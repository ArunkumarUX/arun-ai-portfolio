About

I design the product,
then I build it.

I stay with the work from the first flow to a prototype someone can use. If a person gets stuck, it is not finished.

Read the full story

For twelve years I designed products in banking, healthcare, automotive and commerce. These were flows people had to get right.

I stopped handing over mockups. I learned to prototype in code, connect models and agents, and stay until the product ships.

The work now is executive copilots, multi-agent systems, AI search and investment tools. One Executive AI has been rebranded for five organisations. The shelf holds live products, prototypes and private work under NDA.

The test has not changed. If a person gets stuck, the product is not finished.
